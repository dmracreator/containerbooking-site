Where the market is heading

Built for the forces reshaping global trade.

Three forces define the next decade of forwarding: data, trade complexity and sustainability. ContainerBooking.com is positioned on the right side of all three.

01 · Big data & AI

Turning shipment data into an edge

The move to AI and cloud-based logistics is happening now. Shippers and forwarders that embrace the data era will respond faster to demand, read customer needs earlier, and bring real transparency to the supply chain.

Our structured booking flow captures clean data at the source — the fuel every AI layer will need. Every request makes the next routing decision smarter.

02 · Import duties & complexity

Flexibility as a standing capability

As economies negotiate divergent import taxes, supply chains, costs and compliance grow more complex. Conflicting standards mean shippers will demand far more flexibility from their forwarders.

Our AEO status and in-house customs desk turn that complexity into a service others can’t easily match — clearance stays fast even as the rules shift.

03 · Sustainability & ESG

Moving freight with a smaller footprint

ESG is at the core of our corporate mission, and the environmental side weighs heaviest. Deep-sea shipping keeps global trade moving, but it carries a real carbon cost — one that shippers are increasingly expected to measure, reduce and report.

That is why we back Trees for Transport, a project dedicated to sustainable logistics. It calculates the CO₂ emissions of deep-sea transport and offsets them through carbon credits, giving shippers a concrete way to act on their climate goals and support their ESG reporting.
